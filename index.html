How It Works

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

On-Device Processing

Blind AI performs all camera, image, and audio processing directly on your device. Visual data captured by your camera is never uploaded to our servers or any third party, and is not stored beyond the current session unless you explicitly save a clip.

Information We Collect

We collect only what's needed to operate your account and device: your name, email address, shipping address, and device diagnostics (crash logs, battery, firmware version). We do not collect or store the contents of your camera feed.

Payments & Card Data

Orders are processed through a PCI-DSS compliant payment processor. Blind AI never sees or stores your full credit card number, CVV, or expiry date — this data goes straight from your browser to the payment processor over an encrypted connection and is tokenized before it ever reaches us.

Encryption & Transport Security

All data transmitted between your browser or device and our servers is encrypted in transit using TLS/SSL. Sensitive fields (payment tokens, account credentials) are also encrypted at rest.

Application Security

We follow secure development practices to protect against common web vulnerabilities, including input validation and parameterized queries to prevent injection attacks, regular dependency and security audits, and access controls limiting who can view customer data internally.

Cookies

We use minimal, functional cookies to keep you logged in and remember preferences. We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

Your Rights

You can request a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting our support team.
