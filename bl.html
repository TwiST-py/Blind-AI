How It Works

Three simple steps to a new way of experiencing the world around you.

1

Connect Your Earbuds

Pair any Bluetooth earbuds or AirPods with your smartphone. No proprietary hardware needed — use what you already own.

2

Point the Camera

Hold your phone in front of you or use a chest-mounted holder. Our AI sees what you see in real time.

3

Hear Your World

Receive instant spatial audio descriptions. Objects on your left sound in your left ear, obstacles ahead trigger alerts.

Powerful Features

Built from the ground up for accessibility, speed, and real-world reliability.

Spatial Audio Mapping

3D positional sound places objects exactly where they are in your field of view. Left, right, near, far — your ears become your eyes.

Real-Time Processing

Sub-100ms latency means you get instant feedback. Walk confidently knowing the AI is keeping pace with every step.

Obstacle Detection

Automatically identifies hazards — curbs, poles, stairs, traffic — and alerts you with directional audio warnings.

Smart Narration

Natural language descriptions of scenes, text reading (OCR), face recognition, and color detection — all spoken aloud.

All-Day Battery

Optimized to run efficiently on your phone. No extra devices to charge — just your everyday smartphone and earbuds.

Privacy First

All processing happens on-device. Your visual data never leaves your phone. No cloud, no servers, no compromises.

    Safety notice

    Blind AI is an assistive aid, not a replacement for your primary mobility tools.

    • Keep using your white cane or guide dog — Blind AI adds to them, it does not replace them.
    • No system detects everything. Poor lighting, bad weather, glass surfaces or fast-moving vehicles can be missed.
    • Blind AI is still a prototype: its accuracy and features are being tested and improved.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Short answers to what people ask us most.

    Does Blind AI replace a white cane or guide dog?

    No. Blind AI is an assistive aid: it adds information about your surroundings. Keep using your cane or guide dog together with it.

    What stage is Blind AI at right now?

    We are building and testing the prototype. Next comes a pilot with the first 10 users, then production and delivery.

    How much does it cost?

    $1,000 as a one-time payment, with no subscription or monthly fees. The first 10 users get it for $800.

    Is my camera feed sent to the internet?

    No. Camera, image and audio processing happens on the device itself. Your camera feed is not uploaded to our servers or to anyone else.

    Is there a warranty?

    Yes. The device comes with a 1-year warranty and support, and software updates are free for life.

    Can an organization order several devices?

    Yes. Pricing for several devices is agreed individually, and we can help with training and a pilot program. Request a quote or write to us on WhatsApp.

    Privacy Policy

    Last updated: July 2026

    On-Device Processing

    Blind AI performs all camera, image, and audio processing directly on your device. Visual data captured by your camera is never uploaded to our servers or any third party, and is not stored beyond the current session unless you explicitly save a clip.

    Information We Collect

    We collect only what's needed to operate your account and device: your name, email address, shipping address, and device diagnostics (crash logs, battery, firmware version). We do not collect or store the contents of your camera feed.

    Payments & Card Data

    Orders are processed through a PCI-DSS compliant payment processor. Blind AI never sees or stores your full credit card number, CVV, or expiry date — this data goes straight from your browser to the payment processor over an encrypted connection and is tokenized before it ever reaches us.

    Encryption & Transport Security

    All data transmitted between your browser or device and our servers is encrypted in transit using TLS/SSL. Sensitive fields (payment tokens, account credentials) are also encrypted at rest.

    Application Security

    We follow secure development practices to protect against common web vulnerabilities, including input validation and parameterized queries to prevent injection attacks, regular dependency and security audits, and access controls limiting who can view customer data internally.

    Cookies

    We use minimal, functional cookies to keep you logged in and remember preferences. We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

    Your Rights

    You can request a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting our support team.
